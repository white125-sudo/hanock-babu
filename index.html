AI-powered personal language teacher for English ⇄ Arabic

A conversational AI teacher that explains in English and teaches Arabic with real script, transliteration and harakat. Ships with an SM-2 spaced-repetition engine for vocabulary, text-to-speech that auto-detects and blends English/Arabic voices mid-message, browser-based speech recognition for pronunciation feedback, and AI-generated grammar drills.
